Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Khanshoof is digital signage for cafés, restaurants and shops. This policy explains what information we handle when you use the Khanshoof website (khanshoof.com), the dashboard (app.khanshoof.com), the screen player (play.khanshoof.com) and the Khanshoof app for Android TV, and what we do with it.

Khanshoof is operated by [legal entity], [address], Kuwait ("we", "us"). Questions: hello@khanshoof.com.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: business name, email address, username, language, and your password (stored only as a salted hash). If you sign in with Google: your email and Google account ID (your Google name is offered as the business name, which you can change).To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
Your content: images, videos, PDFs and web addresses you add, and the playlists, schedules, screens and sites you set up.To show it on your screens.
Billing: your plan, payment status and payment references. Card and bank details are entered on the KNET payment page of our payment provider; we never receive them.To run your subscription.
Security records: the IP address and browser of sign-ins and account changes, and failed sign-in attempts.To protect accounts (for example, locking an account after repeated wrong passwords) and to show you an activity log.
Screens: short-lived pairing codes, a random token that identifies each paired screen, and when it was last online.To connect a TV to your account, send it your content and show you whether it is online.

The Android TV app and the player

The TV app shows the Khanshoof player full screen. It stores the screen's token and a copy of your content on the TV so the screen keeps playing when the internet drops. It does not collect information about the people who watch the screen, does not use the TV's advertising ID or other hardware identifiers, and contains no ads or analytics. Pairing codes expire after 5 minutes. Deleting a screen in the dashboard removes its token, and the TV goes back to the pairing screen. The app's data is excluded from Android backups and device transfers.

How we use information

  • to provide Khanshoof: store your content and play it on your screens;
  • to keep accounts secure, including checking new passwords against known data breaches;
  • to send service emails: sign-up codes, and reminders before a trial or subscription ends;
  • to take payments for your plan.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We share only what each one needs to do its job:

  • Cloudflare: delivers our websites and connections securely.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, if you choose it, and web fonts loaded by our pages.
  • Resend: delivers our emails.
  • NiuPay / KNET: process payments.
  • Have I Been Pwned: password breach check. Only the first five characters of a one-way hash of a new password are sent, never the password itself.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions end after a period of inactivity.
  • Pairing codes: 5 minutes.
  • Security records: [audit log retention].
  • Account data and content: while your account is open. After you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within [deletion timeline].

Your choices and rights

You can view and change your account details and content in the dashboard at any time. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, email hello@khanshoof.com from the address on the account.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Accounts are locked temporarily after repeated failed sign-ins.

Children

Khanshoof is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date, and tell account holders by email about significant changes.
