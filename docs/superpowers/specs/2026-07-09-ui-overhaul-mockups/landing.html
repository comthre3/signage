FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Any TV, tablet, or screen with a browser works. Open the player URL, type the pairing code, and you're live.

Does it work in Arabic?

Fully. The dashboard, the player, and support — all bilingual English/Arabic with proper RTL layout.

What happens if the internet drops?

Screens keep playing from their local cache and resync automatically when the connection returns.

How do I pay?

KNET or card, billed monthly in KWD. No annual lock-in.

What happens after the free trial?

You pick a plan, enter a card, and keep going. No auto-charge during the trial — we ask before we bill.
